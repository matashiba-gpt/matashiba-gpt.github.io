Orbit Hitch

Ride a rotating ring, then tap the arena or Release to borrow its momentum and coast outward. Aim for one of the bright hitch points on the next ring. Use tiny left/right thruster taps to bend your coast; fuel is limited. From the outer ring, release into the gold beacon to win.
